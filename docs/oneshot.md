# One-shot player sheets

Printable playbooks and move references for running *Brindlewood Bay* as a single 3–4 hour session.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `print/playbooks.html` | 7 playbooks. Each is one A4 landscape sheet: the character on the front, the basic moves on the back. Print double-sided and flip on the short edge. |
| `print/moves.html` | Table copy: the basic moves page and all Maven moves on one page, with a "Which playbook?" guide. |
| `print/pdf/` | The same sheets, rendered as PDFs. |
| `print/oneshot-data.js` | All the text. Edit it here and both pages pick up the change. |

Rules text is paraphrased from the rulebook.

## How the moves guide without forcing
- Each move keeps the rulebook's mechanics, shortened.
- Style advice is written as **› tips** under a move. A tip is a suggestion, never a rule. Each tip comes from a playtest finding:

| Tip | Playtest finding |
|---|---|
| "Say what you do and which ability, then roll." "Talking is free." | Rounds 1 and 3: tables were confused about when a roll was needed; all four round-3 Keepers named the ability before rolling. |
| Meddling: "The best clues come sideways…" | Rounds 2 and 3: favours and personal stakes produced the best scenes. Pure clue-grabs fell flat. |
| Cozy Move: "Invite someone over." | Round 3: cozy and town scenes were the most remembered. |
| Night Move: "Going anyway is a choice worth making." | Round 3 g4: the Void landed hardest after misses at night on the water. |
| Theorize: "For each Clue, say who, when, and what it proves." "Your theory is what happened." | Rounds 1 and 2: counting clues by feel made Theorize feel arbitrary. |
| Crowns: "Let the disaster play out first." | Round 3: Keepers didn't know how Crowns worked. The rulebook rule is now on every sheet. |

## One-shot changes (house rules, flagged on the sheets)
- **Playbooks.** Six archetypes and one open "Maven" sheet.
  - Each archetype has preset abilities: a +2, +1, 0, 0, −1 spread, which keeps the rulebook's total.
  - Each offers three Maven moves, short name and style lists, and a career.
  - Each has two Cozy Activities that no other playbook shares, so activities can't clash.
  - Why: session zero took 30–45 minutes at all four round-3 tables, and there was no character-creation page.
- **Maven moves.** 18 moves drawn from both rulebook sets. The rulebook says not to mix the sets; this is the exception.
  - Left out: moves that pay off only across a campaign (XP moves, Dark Conspiracy layers, the Consultant, the talking dog) and Frank Dowling.
- **Someone in town.** Each player names a personal stake, with three prompts per playbook. Round 3 found this was the strongest driver of slice-of-life play: shy players opened up once they had a hook.
- **Spotlight replaces XP.** XP does nothing in one session, so the rulebook's End of Session questions become goals to play towards. Mark two. Tick one when you do it on screen, and spend a tick for advantage. Advancements are dropped.
- **Clues on the table.** A clue counts for Theorize once it's on the table. Keeping it secret is allowed. Why: round-3 g3 had a hidden clue and no rule for it.
- **Table knowledge vs Maven knowledge.** Players pass on information through the story. Why: round 3, g3 and g4.
- **Crown of the Void** keeps all five boxes. Being lost to the Void is a fine way to end a one-shot.

## Keeper notes for a one-shot
- Session zero should take 15 minutes:
  1. Hand out playbooks.
  2. Players work through the numbered steps ① to ⑧.
  3. Each player gives their "Someone in town". Write each one on a card.
- **Complexity 6** for a 3-hour slice-of-life session. Round-3 tables found 4–10 clues. At Complexity 8, none could Theorize.
- Tell the players the Complexity at the start, and write each clue on a card as it lands.
- Call Theorize with about 40 minutes left, even if the case feels unfinished.
- Play Crown scenes in the last 10–15 minutes.

## Printing
Open the HTML in a browser and choose **Print**. Use A4 landscape at 100% scale with no extra margins (the sheets set 8 mm). The `?pb=nurse` URL parameter (also `starlet`, `busybody`, `teacher`, `salt`, `spiritualist`, `maven`) preselects one playbook. On US Letter, choose "Fit to page".
