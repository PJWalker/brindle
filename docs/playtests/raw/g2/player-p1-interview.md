Stepping out of character. Here's my honest feedback as the playtester.

**1. Best parts.** The fish market scene was the highlight for me. The Rolex set ninety minutes off, Finch saying "wound and SET," and the cod with the rat in its mouth all felt like real evidence I had to interpret myself. Etienne's panic was a good NPC beat. Harriet's chemistry background gave me a clear lens, and the rolled rug with porcelain slivers felt like something a retired teacher would actually notice.

**2. Confusion.** Mostly about mechanics. I didn't know when a question was a Meddling Move versus free. I asked several times (Presence or Reason for Sorensen? Vitality for the road?) and got different answers. Knowing whether a Harriet question to Finch was free was never settled either. I also wasn't sure whether an OOC question about a roll counted as a turn.

**3. Assumptions I made.** I assumed Krause's will and Friday deadline were real facts, not just Sorensen's claim. I assumed the 12:40 launch and the routing number 3-27-9-14 were connected. I assumed Duchess was a witness of some kind. I also invented why Harriet knew the Corporal's name would matter.

**4. Felt off.** Sara's diary was a late reveal that didn't connect to anything we had. The pistol and David's gouged photo were never resolved, so they sat as loose threads. The chanting and violet light were never explained, which made the ending feel like it was setting up a sequel rather than closing the case.

**5. Theorize.** The roll felt generous. We ignored four clues and still hit 12+, which made the solution feel earned but a bit too easy. The clues fit Allison and Andrew well, but I suspect they'd fit almost any wealthy-family theory.

**6. Unfollowed leads.** The cod's rat, the guest list, Sorensen's card, Emily in the cabin, and the robed crew's Portuguese all had no payoff. I wanted to follow Emily most.

**7. Pacing.** Good in the middle, then the back half sprinted from the docks to the night raid to the theory roll with little breathing room.

**8. One change.** Resolve the violet light and the chanting. Give one loose thread a payoff, ideally David's gouged face, so the family dynamic feels finished.
