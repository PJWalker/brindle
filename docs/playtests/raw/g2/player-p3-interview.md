Stepping out of character. Here's my honest feedback as the player.

**1. What worked best?** The Candlelight loft opening and Hollis's log were strong. Hollis flipping the logbook open with "Judge, in the margin..." and the penciled 3-27-9-14 gave me a real puzzle to carry through the whole session, and it paid off when Bunny saw the same string on Sara's bank form. The Sorensen scene on the dock was the best NPC moment: a debt collector who is polite, menacing and happy to give you a lift.

**2. Where was I stuck?** Mostly around the Regal Lady. I didn't know what Allison actually wanted from us, or whether a "Mavens' visit" to the yacht had any real leverage. Constance's Presence rolls against Allison kept feeling like they were bouncing off a door, and I wasn't sure whether to keep pushing or switch to another lead. The "Emily" thread also went quiet after the first reveal; I didn't know where to take it.

**3. What did I have to assume?** Quite a lot. I assumed the 12:40 launch was Sorensen's man, not a family member. I assumed Sara's diary was about her mother. I didn't know who the "Warrior's Ball" was for beyond the charity angle, or whether Tommy Gage was a suspect or a red herring. I also filled in why Duchess was in the cold store.

**4. What felt off?** The chanting, the pale-coated mourners and the violet light never connected to anything, and the Keeper's note that they "don't fit" made it feel like they were decoration. Tiago and the tattoos that "ripple" was intriguing but went nowhere. Andrew's "Madam only wanted him to stop" in the epilogue came out of nowhere for me.

**5. The theory and the roll.** The roll felt generous. Eleven clues against a flat Complexity of 6 meant the answer was basically guaranteed once we had enough. The theory felt earned in outline, but it was built more on my reading of the family's body language than on hard evidence, so I'd have liked a clue that actually ruled someone out.

**6. Leads I wanted but couldn't follow:** Nadine's Gazette angle, Sorensen's Boston number (it went to voicemail), and the blood-soaked rug's porcelain (we never identified the vase).

**7. Pacing:** Good early, but the Regal Lady and Inn section dragged a little while we circled Allison, and the night raid was rushed.

**8. One change:** Give the chanting and violet light a concrete clue thread, even a small one. As it stands, they're the most atmospheric element and the least connected.
