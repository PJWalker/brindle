Stepping out of character. Here's my honest feedback as the player of Bunny, with Jess as the bold one.

1. **Best:** The Sara thread. Bunny's bus-route memory of Sara correcting her transfer count set up a real emotional hook, and then Sara's whispered "The study. Tonight." and the oilskin diary at the end made her feel like the one who was actually trying to help. The Sorensen "death doesn't cancel a debt, it changes the address" line was also excellent; it gave me a villain with menace and a clue at once.

2. **Confusing:** Mostly the mechanics of "what to do next" once we split up. Three scenes at once meant I didn't always know whether Bunny's actions were affecting Harriet's or Constance's threads. The Theorize step came late and felt like it arrived without warning; I didn't realise we'd been collecting clues for a theory until the GM said so. I'd have liked a list of open leads at the start of each scene.

3. **Assumed:** Who Tiago was, what the chanting is, and whether the violet light was connected to the Void or just local colour. I also assumed Duchess's bowl meant someone was feeding her, and that Allison's "lawyer's letter" was a real threat rather than bluff. Nobody told me the timeline of Albert's death; I worked it out from the watch and the 12:40 launch.

4. **Off:** The Void elements felt disconnected from the murder plot. The chanting, the praying townsfolk and the violet light never got explained, and the epilogue just leaves them hanging. The .22 pistol, the gouged photo and the diary "I can't take it anymore" were never integrated into the theory, so the "unexplained" pile felt bigger than it should. Also, Hollis's attacker, and why Sorensen would leave the orange peel as a signature, were never resolved.

5. **Theorize:** The roll felt fair, but the solution was too easy to assemble. Allison and Andrew were the obvious suspects by the time we'd seen the stateroom; the will and the receipts pointed at them neatly. The clues were specific enough to mean something, but a few (the cod, the guest list) were discarded as loose threads, which felt like the game telling us our reasoning was right rather than testing it.

6. **Wanted:** Cpl. Tommy Gage's crossed-out name and the guest list. Bunny never got a real chance to question him, and the Ball felt like a missed scene.

7. **Pacing:** Fast in the middle (yacht, docks, Inn), but the Theorize step was rushed.

8. **Change:** Give each Maven a clear personal lead early, and tie the Void into the murder plot instead of leaving it as atmosphere.
