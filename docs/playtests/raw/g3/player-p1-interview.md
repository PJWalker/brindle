Stepping out of character. Here is my honest feedback as the Mavis player.

1. **Best part:** The Hollis launch log and Sorensen at the Inn. Sorensen gave a clean, creepy clue ("Death doesn't cancel a debt, friend. It just changes the address") and the two silhouettes on the aft deck. That scene made the suspect list feel real. The cold-store scene was also good: the Rolex reading 9:30 against a real 11:00 was a satisfying concrete clue.

2. **Confusion:** The biggest problem was not knowing what the Theorize step needed. I didn't know we'd be counting clues until the list appeared, so I spent the whole game collecting things without knowing which ones mattered. The "Void" category was never explained. I had no idea whether the pollock, the Point prayer group, and the shifting tattoos were red herrings, a separate plot, or a warning, and I had no way to find out.

3. **Assumptions I made:** Who Sorensen was, what "Void" meant, whether the Sheriff's office had any records, and whether Mavis could walk around the yacht freely. I also assumed the paternity sheet and the love letter were meant to be connected, which turned out to be a guess.

4. **Felt off:** Gage's timeline ("a quarter to twelve") and Emily's 11:46 message were fine, but Sorensen's launch left at 12:40, so he was ashore while the killing happened. That made him a red herring in plain sight, and I never got a chance to press him on it. Andrew's "Bordeaux" lie was good. The Sheriff being both useless and in possession of a dead man's notes felt like it contradicted itself.

5. **Theorize:** The solution felt earned in the epilogue, but the roll felt arbitrary. I counted twelve clues and the dice made it succeed, but the Void clues had no place to go, so the final count felt like a formality. The confession answered the question rather than rewarding the theory.

6. **Unfollowed leads:** The Caymans account, the "T." lover (Gage), and the foundation books Albert was going to expose. I never had a scene to chase any of them.

7. **Pacing:** Good in the middle, slow in the morning (the Inn and the fish market took a lot of rolling for one clue each). The Sheriff's office and the yacht lunch felt rushed.

8. **One change:** Tell the players early what Theorize requires and what "Void" means, so we can aim our investigation.
