Stepping out of character. Here's my honest feedback as the player of Agnes, with Priya's hat on.

**1. What worked best:** The Regal Lady scenes were the highlight. The telescope watch, then the launch with the diamond earring, then the supper where Allison's single earring and Sara's diary line came out together. That's a good escalating sequence, and the "one earring, one ear" detail was a nice clue that felt physical. Duchess the samoyed was also a great hook. Her growl at the yacht and the blood in her fur made the dog feel like a character, not a prop.

**2. Confusion:** Mostly I wasn't sure what counted as a clue versus a note. When the Sheriff bagged the pistol, I didn't know whether Agnes had earned it or whether it was just a cutaway. The Theorize rules came late, and I had to ask twice whether a clue counted once it was found or only once shared. Early on, I wasn't sure which leads were open: the harbormaster, the fish market, the yacht, Doc Finch, and the Gazette were all listed, but I never got a clear sense of which one was the real bottleneck.

**3. What I assumed:** I assumed the town had a working timeline: Albert's death, the 8:50 launch, the 11:40 return, the tide. I also assumed Andrew was the household's servant who'd been sweet on Allison, based on Bev's gossip, and that the chanting under the hull was connected to the Void. Neither was spelled out.

**4. What felt off:** Several clues (the samoyed's blood, the chanting, the "Void," David's paternity, Emily's DMs, the Sheriff's struck name) were left outside the theory, so they felt like dangling threads rather than red herrings. The final epilogue's "knocks twice and is satisfied" landed, but the chanting never got a payoff. The Sheriff's behavior also swung between hostile and helpful without a clear reason.

**5. Theorize:** The roll felt earned, since the table did the work of agreeing on a story. But nine clues against Complexity 6 was generous, and the "culprit confesses" ending felt a little neat. I'd have liked a clue that actively contradicted the theory so we had to argue around it.

**6. Leads I wanted:** Sorensen the debt collector, the Warrior's Ball guest list, the engine room, Hollis's launch log for the full night, and what Etienne was really hiding.

**7. Pacing:** Good overall. The split-party rounds moved quickly. The middle stretch between the docks and the yacht felt slightly rushed.

**8. One change:** Give the chanting and the Void a clear hook early, so they tie into the theory rather than floating outside it.
