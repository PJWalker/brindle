Stepping out of character. Here is my candid feedback as Dana.

1. **Best part:** The Fish Market cold store. Finding the watch stopped at 9:30 on Albert's wrist, with Etienne hovering at my shoulder, was the strongest moment for me. It gave me a concrete, checkable clue and a live complication at the same time. The ice block of certificates with the 14-27-09-33 slip was a close second. Both felt like things an auditor would actually notice.

2. **Confusion:** The Theorize step was the murkiest part. I knew I needed clues that "fit," but the rules didn't say how strictly. Sorting clues into a column helped, but I was never sure whether a clue like Emily's DMs "counted" if I could argue it. Investigation options were clear enough at the docks, less so once the family was on deck and the Sheriff started steering the group.

3. **Assumptions I made:** I assumed the Krauses' money came from a trust Edith had audited, which was never stated beyond Sara's statements. I also assumed the Sheriff's guest-list line meant he'd been struck off the Warrior's Ball by Albert, which the GM confirmed only in-scene. I invented the idea that Andrew "kept the household clock" as a motive for the time lie; that came from the table, not the world.

4. **What felt off:** Sorensen, Etienne's debt, and the stranger with the orange were set up as a strong thread and then dropped. Did they ever connect to the Krauses or the will? I also never learned what the chanting or the knocks are, which the closing round called a "town secret," yet we got no hook. David's paternity and "Marcus" got one line each and then vanished.

5. **Theorize:** The roll of 11 felt generous but earned enough, because nine named clues were tied to a single struggle aft. The problem is it felt like we'd been told the answer. Andrew's confession came out of nowhere, and the ending gave no reason to doubt the table's theory.

6. **Unfollowed leads:** The router logs and the lawyer's name, the pistol's serial number, Duchess's blood and the blue stationery note from "T." I wanted to chase the lawyer, but no scene gave me a way to reach him.

7. **Pacing:** The first half was brisk, but the split-up rounds made it hard to tell what the others had learned until the relay summaries.

8. **One change:** Give the outside threads (Sorensen, the Void, the chanting) at least one scene that connects them back to the will, so the unexplained clues are more than texture.
