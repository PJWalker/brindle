Stepping out of character, happy to be candid.

1. **Best moments:** The forecastle supper was the highlight. Allison's hand lingering on Andrew's sleeve, Andrew's "Ten-eighteen, madam," and the receipts for the Warrior's Ball gave me a whole room of people to read. Emily's bedroom, with the DMs and the blue stationery signed "T," felt like a genuinely good thread. Hollis, Bev and the staff gossip made the town feel lived-in.

2. **Confusion:** Mostly I felt I could investigate freely, which was a relief. The stuck point was the middle: after the yacht, I had about fifteen loose threads (Sorensen, T, the chanting, David's "not my real father," the Sheriff's struck-off name) and no sense of which ones were meant to connect. Lolly's instinct is to chase the dramatic, so I drifted toward the will and the affair because they were the most vivid, not because I knew they were central.

3. **Assumptions I made:** I assumed Albert's will change was the motive without being told why it mattered financially. I assumed Andrew and Allison's relationship was the intended hidden truth, since Bev's gossip pointed there. I didn't know what the chanting or "Void" meant, so I left it out.

4. **Off-feeling:** The rolls were generous, and clues often arrived with a complication that just led to another clue. The Theorize step felt abrupt. We were asked to pick one culprit and then a roll decided "right with a wrinkle," but I'm not sure the wrinkle was ever explained. The epilogue confession from Andrew also felt like it arrived from nowhere; I'd have liked a clue that pointed to him specifically.

5. **Theorize:** The solution felt earned enough, but also a little too tidy given how many loose threads were left outside the theory. Counting clues felt mechanical; I had to guess which ones "fit" by feel.

6. **Missed leads:** The Sorensen debt thread, the 9:26 router logs (I asked, but no answer came), the frozen certificate code 14-27-09-33, and the ENGINE ROOM hatch all went nowhere for me.

7. **Pacing:** Good overall. The fish market and docks section was brisk. The gap between the yacht and the Gull & Anchor felt rushed.

8. **One change:** Give each unresolved thread a clear "this is a red herring" or "this matters" signal so the table isn't guessing which of fifteen threads to build the theory on.
