Table: Ellen (Dot Pruitt, 71, retired piano teacher), Frank (Lu Abernathy, 68, retired postmistress), Jo (Agnes Quill, 74, retired court stenographer, dawn cold-water swimmer). Ellen/Frank want their Mavens inseparable; Jo wants solo scenes and secrets.

## What I was trying to do, scene by scene

**0:00 Pitch / session zero.** Delivered the "slice of life, take initiative, no rounds" pitch in my own words, then lines and veils. The seed has no character-creation rules and no playbooks. I improvised: five abilities, assign +2/+1/+1/0/-1, a name, age, former career, cozy activity. Asked for an establishing answer, one personal stake each, and a paint answer for the loft. This took 45 minutes with three players (with a snack break), which ate a quarter of the session.

**0:45 Scene 1, loft + Sheriff.** Open on the hook. Dalrymple is "guarded", so I made the file require a Meddle (Dot, Presence). It was a 10+, so I gave c3 immediately. Probably right, but note this makes c3 the first clue in the first five minutes. Used the Establishing Answer by having Emily text Dot at the end of the scene. Deliberately framed an exit for Jo: she asked to go home alone and I said yes without forcing a group.

**1:10 Scene 2, diner.** Emily seeks out Dot/Lu (Establishing Answer: Emily appears on screen, first day, per r-townlife). Played the Emily scene quiet and let silences sit. Added the Bev/Etienne chowder gossip and Dot's prom flame Hollis at the counter as pure town life. Lu noticed Emily's screen: 7-9. I gave c22 and the complication was Emily realising she'd been seen; hard choice was whether to promise secrecy. Frank improvised a clever "I won't lie for you, I just won't volunteer" which I'd never have planned.

**1:40 Scene 3, the Point at dusk.** Solo scene for Jo. Used the lighthouse description (ebb tide, Gil, white dog on the rocks, kneeling spot) and v4. 10+ got v4 and the spiral on the kneeler's wrist (v3 flavour, not counted as a second clue). Duchess is clearly Gil's muddy white dog; I let Jo see the collar tag and keep it to herself. Ticked t4 to 1.

**2:05 Scene 4, the Point, short.** Resolved the cliffhanger with a polite kneeler returning her towel and using her name. No roll; Jo turned it into a Void moment and bailed on the swim.

**2:10 Scene 5, fish market.** Used Etienne's life first (wedding, chowder, "need a wise old woman on the panel"), then Finch. Lu offered to judge the chowder in Finch's place, so Finch spilled all of his autopsy facts with no roll because a favour opened the door. Dot distracted Etienne while Lu looked at the watch: 7-9, c4 plus a complication (Etienne cuts off access; t2 to 1). Sorensen walks out the back peeling an orange in one spiral.

**2:30 Scene 6, Agnes and Bev.** Pure Jo-driven errand (pies for Gil). Miss on Composure. Keeper move: reveal an unwelcome truth plus put a Maven in debt. Bev says Sorensen is asking after her, and gives her twelve free pies (a favour that is a debt). Jo took the Insecure condition herself.

**2:42 Scene 7, loft.** Reunion: players tell the story in character. Ticked t1 and t5 for time passing. Introduced Theo (the grandson from Ellen's stake) as the cozy interruption. Ended on Hollis's launch lights heading out.

**2:50 Wrap-up.** End-of-session questions.

## Prep gaps / contradictions found in the seed
1. **No character creation.** No playbook, no ability values, no "cozy activity" or "Condition" lists, no XP advance menu, no Crown of the Void rules. I improvised everything. At a table with a 3-hour slot this is 45 minutes of improvisation.
2. **Gerald Prentiss vs Gerald Pratt.** The victim's "hides" says Albert was meeting "Gerald Pratt" on Monday; Prentiss is everywhere else.
3. **Prudence Lavoie vs Philippa Hale.** Two event managers at the Brindlewood Inn with near-identical entries (one in the setting, one in the mystery). Hale is the one who knows who crossed out c9.
4. **Allison and Andrew timeline.** Allison hides "Andrew: twenty years"; Andrew hides "thirty-five years of devotion"; the relationship list says "35 years" and solutions use 35 but David is 34.
5. **Timeline words.** Hook says "several nights ago", backstory says "three nights ago", and the Warrior's Ball is "a week away" / "next week" but Saturday night in the setting happenings. Meanwhile the Festival is Saturday and the Sheriff's election is a September primary. I never got a clear "what day is it today" from the seed. I picked Tuesday and ticked t5 per day without knowing what "invitations reprinted" means for a Ball that is meant to be days away.
6. **Sorensen's times.** Backstory: aboard from 9:50pm (launch) and 12:20 radio call, ashore 12:40. NPC entry: aboard "10pm to 12:25am". Fine, but there are three versions.
7. **v1, v5, v6 have no notes.** The three fish and shadow Void clues have no mundane thread and no scene.
8. **c16 as a bearing.** "3-27-9-14" can't be a compass bearing in any obvious way (27 degrees?). The Void thread says "read as a bearing from the lamp room" and gives no convention.
9. **Threat t2 trigger.** "Tick it on misses at the fish market and at scene changes." Which scene changes? Every one? I ticked on the fish-market complication only and let the clock sit otherwise.
10. **t5 "once per in-game day"** needs a day clock the seed doesn't set up.
11. **Clue budget vs Complexity.** The seed says 10-12 clues by Theorize and Complexity 8. With the Theorize formula (2d6 + clues - 8) a table needs about 8 clues to be at even odds of a 7-9. We had 4 clues at 3 hours. Theorize was never reachable, and I didn't push.
12. **c3 is available instantly.** The Sheriff's file lists c3's location. A 10+ at minute 20 gives it away and starts everyone on "they rehearsed" before any other lead exists.
13. **Establishing question vs Krause adults.** Fine ("several years ago", the child grown). Ellen chose Emily at age 9 (14 years ago), which the seed allows but the question text says "several". I allowed it.
14. **Duchess is already at the Point** per Gil's life entry and c20's notes, but c20's `where` also lists loc-light. When Jo kept the dog secret, I couldn't tell whether c20 counts as found.
15. **Autopsy facts are not clues.** Everything Finch says (head wound, bruise, BAC) is the most solid evidence in the whole seed and is not on the clue list. A table using them at Theorize would have nothing to tick.

## Rulings improvised
- A favour from a Maven opens an NPC with no roll (Lu's chowder offer got Finch talking).
- A kind of "table knowledge" rule: Jo, Ellen and Frank hear everything at the table. I asked each, whenever a Maven wasn't in the scene, to say whether her Maven knew. Jo did so every time; Ellen reached once (the spiral) and I refused to confirm.
- The Void tick on a Void clue is automatic (t4 +1).
- Conditions: Jo picked Insecure for herself on a miss; seed lists Conditions but no mechanical effect, so none applied.

## Moments the players surprised me
- Lu offering to judge the chowder contest on impulse, giving Finch and Etienne a whole new interaction.
- Ellen's six-minute derail into a chowder recipe, which I let run.
- Jo withholding the dog from the group; it gave the reunion scene real friction.
- Dot ignoring Hollis in the diner for the whole scene, then four lines of dialogue that did all the work.
- Frank spontaneously giving Lu a dead husband, Harold.

## What worked / didn't
- **Slice of life:** worked once the players had personal stakes; each stake (Hollis, Bev's cake, dahlias vs Finch, Theo) produced a scene on its own. Roughly three of seven scenes were non-murder on their face: Bev and the pies, Theo in the loft, and the chowder tangent, with Hollis at the diner.
- **No rounds:** worked easily. The table interrupts each other fine; I never went around the table.
- **Splitting:** easy with Jo (she wanted it); Ellen/Frank resisted and had to be split by my framing (the pie run).
- **Pacing:** 45 minutes of chargen plus slow cozy scenes left too little time for the case.
