# Brindlewood Bay — "Dad Overboard" — Round 3, Table G3 (simulated)

Keeper: quiet, lets silences sit. Players: Ellen, Frank (married; want their Mavens to be best friends), Jo (loner, solo, secrets).
Table clock runs 0:00 to 3:00 including character creation and pitch.

---

## 0:00 — Pitch, safety, session zero, character creation (45 min, incl. a snack break and a long argument about who gets the good chair)

**Keeper** (after a long pause, looking at each of them): "Before we start, I'll tell you how I'd like this to go. You're the Mavens. This is a slice of life: your characters have lives, errands, grudges and hobbies that have nothing to do with the dead man on the fish-market table. Go where you want. Take the initiative. There are no rounds, no turn order. If something grabs you, say so, interrupt each other, jump in. If you want to go off alone, go. I'll cut between you. When you push for something someone wants hidden, roll 2d6 plus an ability and I'll tell you which one before the dice land. A success earns a clue. Nothing's been decided about who did it. When you're ready you'll put a theory together, and every clue your theory actually *explains* helps the roll. If the roll's good, your theory is what happened. Some clues are just strange, not suspicious: those are the Void. You can weave them in or leave them. Lines and veils first: anything you never want at the table?"

**Ellen:** OOC: no harm to animals on screen, please. Frank's fine with the rest.
**Jo:** OOC: nothing about kids being hurt. Otherwise I'm good.
**Keeper:** "Duchess the dog comes into this; she'll be fine. Noted. X-card's there if you need it."
**Frank:** OOC: "Do we need to roll up stats? Is there a sheet?" *(Keeper: the seed has the five abilities but no playbook, so improvised. See gm-notes.)*

**Character creation (improvised).** Keeper: "Five abilities: Vitality, Composure, Reason, Presence, Sensitivity. Assign +2, +1, +1, 0, -1 however you like, and give me a name, an age, what you did, and a cozy activity."

- **Ellen: Dorothea "Dot" Pruitt, 71**, retired piano teacher. Cozy: she plays hymns badly on purpose to annoy the choir. Presence +2, Reason +1, Composure +1, Sensitivity 0, Vitality -1.
- **Frank: Lucille "Lu" Abernathy, 68**, retired postmistress. Cozy: gardening, dahlias in particular. Sensitivity +2, Composure +1, Vitality +1, Reason 0, Presence -1.
- **Jo: Agnes Quill, 74**, retired court stenographer, widowed, lives alone. Cozy: swims in the cold water off the rocks at dawn. Vitality +2, Reason +1, Composure +1, Sensitivity 0, Presence -1.

**Ellen** (laughing): "Dot and Lu have been best friends since the Carter administration. They split the crossword and fight over who finishes it."
**Frank:** "Lu would absolutely let Dot copy her tomatoes."
**Jo:** "Agnes comes to the meetings. She sits in the corner with the good chair and takes notes nobody asked for."
**Keeper:** (a long silence, then) "Agnes takes notes. Good."

**Establishing question: "You met one of the Krause children several years ago. What were the circumstances?"**
- **Ellen:** Dot taught Emily piano for one summer when the yacht came through, Emily was nine. "She was ferocious about scales and then just stopped playing. I never knew why." *(Emily it is.)*
- **Frank:** Lu was the postmistress when Sara, then twenty-something, came in every day for a week to mail postcards to the same Boston address and never put a return address. "Lu figured she was writing to someone she wasn't supposed to."
- **Jo:** Agnes took David's deposition in a Boston gallery-fraud suit eleven years ago. "He drew a caricature of the judge during testimony and Agnes typed it as '[sketching]'."

**Personal stakes (Keeper asks each for one).**
- **Ellen:** "Dot went to the prom with Hollis Crane in 1969. He still hasn't forgiven her for leaving early with the band." (old flame; harbormaster)
- **Frank:** "Lu and Dr. Finch have a dahlia feud. His took second to hers at the county fair and he claims the judging was rigged."
- **Jo:** "Bev Tuttle ruined Agnes's wedding cake in 1974. Agnes has never said a word about it to anyone and will never forgive her." *Keeper: "How did she ruin it?" Jo: "Sat on it. Deliberately." Table dies laughing.*
- **Ellen** adds: Dot's grandson Theo, 19, is "somewhere between a gap year and a crisis" and may turn up.

**Paint the loft (Keeper asks the question).** Ellen: Dot has a battered upright against the wall she's not allowed to play. Frank: Lu keeps a window box of dahlia tubers over the radiator. Jo: Agnes has a steno machine under a dust cover by her chair, "which no one has ever asked about."

## 0:45 — Scene 1: The loft, the Sheriff (25 min)

*Candlelight Booksellers, top floor, Tuesday around 4pm. Cinnamon tea, Margo pressing a paperback into someone's hand, rain on the skylight.*

Keeper narrates the end of the weekly meeting. Margo is mid-rant about Seaside Reads (the pop-up shop on the wharf) and hands Lu a "Seaside Reads is stealing my customers" petition. Then boots on the stairs: **Sheriff Dalrymple**, hat a size too small, in the doorway.

**Dalrymple:** "Believe you me, no one is more frustrated about having to ask for help from a bunch of old biddies than I am." He gives the hook: Albert Krause, financier, hauled out of the bay dead; the family says he was drunk and fell; the yacht is a quarter mile out; the body is in the cold store at Beauregard's fish market. "I'm not saying foul play. I'm saying I can't prove it isn't."

**Frank/Lu:** "Is there a Mrs. Dalrymple who would like this settled before the primary?" *(laughter)* The Sheriff hitches his belt.
**Ellen/Dot:** "Wyman, I taught you 'Chopsticks'. You played it with your elbows. Show us the file." 
**Jo/Agnes** (OOC, quietly): "Agnes isn't going to say anything. She's going to look at his shoes." *Keeper: "Muddy at the heel. Dried salt rings." Jo: "Good."*

**Dot Meddles** (persuade / old-teacher charm): **Presence +2. Roll 5+6 = 13, full success.** Sheriff grudgingly slides over the thin file. Keeper hands a clue, described without interpretation:

> **Clue c3 (found, Sheriff's file).** Four family statements that say "about eleven" in nearly the same words. Andrew's says "11:00 precisely."

**Ellen:** OOC "That's too similar. They rehearsed." **Keeper:** "I haven't said anything about that." (Players arguing it out aloud is good table talk.)

Dalrymple also admits: "The Krauses' lawyers say they can go any day. Give me something solid by tomorrow." *(Threat t1 stays at 0; Mavens just got a deadline, not a tick.)*

**Establishing answer plays.** As the Sheriff leaves, a text-message ping goes off in Dot's purse: an unknown number. "Miss Pruitt? It's Emily Krause. You taught me piano. Can I talk to you? Not on the boat." **Ellen:** "Dot wants to go right now." **Frank:** "Lu is coming with her." **Jo:** "Agnes is going home to feed her cat and doesn't tell them that she's going somewhere after." (Jo wants alone-time. Keeper agrees: the others leave.)

**Table:** Ellen and Frank immediately want to go together; Jo's off alone, which the Keeper had planned to propose anyway.

## 1:10 — Scene 2: The Gull & Anchor, a back booth (30 min)

*Cut to Dot and Lu, Tuesday about 5:30pm. The diner is half full; the supper crowd is arguing about the moorings vote. Bev Tuttle at full volume: "HON! Sit anywhere! Not there, that's Hollis's!"*

**Keeper** (framing): "Emily's text said 'not on the boat'. She came in by Hollis's launch, hoodie up, and is sitting in the back booth with headphones round her neck, typing. Bev is already topping up her coffee and Emily hasn't ordered any."

**Ellen/Dot:** "Dot sits and says nothing. Just puts her hands flat on the table the way she used to on the piano lid." *(Keeper lets the silence sit; Emily types, then stops.)*
**Emily** (monotone): "You're older. ...Sorry. That came out wrong." **Dot:** "It's allowed."
Emily explains, haltingly: she doesn't think her father fell. Everyone on the yacht is acting like it's "an event they were supposed to rehearse." She asks Dot if she still has the piano. She *doesn't* want to talk about the yacht yet. **Frank/Lu** orders pie for all of them and "pushes it at Emily without comment." (Emily eats it. Small win. Establishing answer used: Emily on screen, first day.)

**Beat of town life.** Bev, passing: "Hon, that boy Rémy has asked me to taste his father's chowder *before the contest*. Before! Like I'm some kind of spy." Lu asks if Etienne's chowder is any good. "It's got *thyme* in it. In *chowder*. I'm calling the sheriff." The Sheriff's current worry list does not include chowder.
At the counter, Hollis Crane in a yellow slicker, pipe unlit, eating alone. **Ellen/Dot:** "Dot ignores him very hard." **Frank:** "Lu gives her a look." Hollis, without turning: "Dorothea." Dot: "Hollis." Long silence. Hollis: "Vote's Thursday. Moorings. You still got opinions, ain't you?" Dot: "Always." Hollis: "Good." That's the whole exchange. *(Keeper's note: nailed the old-flame beat in four lines; Ellen is delighted.)*

**Back to Emily.** She types, closes the laptop halfway, reopens it to answer a DM. Lu is watching.
**Frank/Lu** (OOC): "Lu looks at the screen. Not rudely. She's a postmistress; she's read people's return addresses upside down for forty years."
**Keeper:** "That's Meddling: noticing. Sensitivity?" **Frank:** "Yes, +2." *(Keeper says the ability aloud before the dice hit.)*
**Lu Meddles: Sensitivity +2. Roll 3+2 = 7, mixed.** Clue with a complication.

> **Clue c22 (found, Emily's laptop).** A wire-transfer confirmation, timestamped 12:07am the night Albert died: a Krause Capital account to an account in the Cayman Islands.

**Complication:** Emily catches Lu reading. She slams the laptop and goes white. "Please don't tell anyone. Not the Sheriff. Not Mom. Please." Hard choice: promise, or not. **Frank:** "Lu promises. But she says, 'I'm not going to lie for you, dear, I'll just not volunteer.'" **Emily** (grateful, still scared): "I need to get back before they notice the launch." She leaves cash on the table that Bev refuses with outrage. As Emily goes out, **Deputy Pelletier** on the dock gives her a wave that's much too friendly.

**Table talk (table knowledge):** Jo, at the table, hears all of this. She's not in the scene and will not use it for Agnes (she says OOC, "Agnes doesn't know that").
**Ellen** (OOC, to Frank): "Is the money hers or her father's? She was typing when it went through." **Frank:** "I'm not telling you what I think yet." (They are enjoying being allies and holding back theories from each other, which the Keeper notes with amusement.)

## 1:40 — Scene 3: Brindle Point, dusk (25 min)

*Cut to Agnes alone. Tuesday just before sunset, thin rain stopped, light going copper. Jo decided at the end of Scene 1 that she's going to take her swimsuit and "the long way home."*

**Jo/Agnes:** "She doesn't tell Dot and Lu. She tells herself she's going to swim." **Keeper:** "The path along the rocks under the lighthouse. Salt-stiff weeds, the foam line high." **Jo:** "She knows this spot. She swam here yesterday. And last month, at dawn, she heard something she hasn't told anyone." *(Jo's secret: Agnes heard chanting off the Point at dawn a month ago and has told no one.)* **Keeper:** nothing; lets the silence sit; Jo smiles.

Keeper frames: the keeper's cottage with a lit window; a muddy white dog sitting, perfectly still, on a flat rock where the tide has left a dark stain. A shape in the cardigan behind: Gil Wexford with binoculars, polishing them on his sleeve.
**Gil** (apologetic): "I'm sorry about the dog hair. She won't come in. She sits right where they kneel at dusk."
**Jo/Agnes:** "Who kneels?" **Gil:** "Oh. I shouldn't say. People. Folks." and goes back to talking about the lamp, which is broken, and how he needs a dozen pies by Saturday for the bake sale and cannot bake. *(Town life: Agnes is given a favour-door. Jo: "Agnes doesn't bake. She notes it for later.")*

At dusk, the group comes. **Keeper:** "Seven or eight figures coming down the path from the road in ones and twos. No flashlights. A woman in a church cardigan. A man in oilskins. Two younger men she has never seen, in a good dock-worker's jacket."
**Jo:** "Agnes slips down into the rocks. Climbing. Quiet. She wants to see their faces without being seen." **Keeper:** "That's sneaking: Vitality." **Jo:** "+2."
**Agnes Meddles: Vitality +2. Roll 6+6 = 14, full success.**

> **Clue v4 (Void, found at the Point).** A group of people gathering and praying near the spot where Mr. Krause's body was found. They will not interact with anyone, and disperse if someone gets too pushy.

Keeper gives it vividly, without interpreting: they kneel in a rough line facing the water, the dog beside them, and their words are not any hymn she knows; a low, rhythmic *a-ha, a-ha*, in time with the swell. One of the kneelers has a spiral on the inside of his wrist, and she can't quite pin it to where it was a moment ago. When the chant ends the water at the foot of the rocks has gone very still and the foam is moving *away* from the shore.
She sees the dog has a collar with a tag: a name and a Boston phone number, caked in mud.

**Keeper:** "It's a samoyed. Under the mud." **Jo** (quietly): "Agnes isn't telling Dot and Lu about the dog yet." **Keeper:** "That's your call." *(Duchess seen but not counted as c20; Jo is holding it back at the table, and the Keeper notes this conflicts with the 'shared clue list' guidance in r-split.)*

**Threat t4 ticks to 1**: dead fish on the tide line; the prayer group is larger than it was last month.

Cut on Agnes crouched among the rocks as the last kneeler turns and looks directly into the dark where she is.
