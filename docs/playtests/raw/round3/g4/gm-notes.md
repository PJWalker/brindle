## Pre-session prep gaps (things I needed and didn't have)
1. **No character-creation rules.** Seed lists five attributes and xpMax 5 / crownsMax 7 but not how to build a Maven, assign scores, pick a cozy activity, or what XP advances are. Improvised +2/+1/0/0/-1. Took ~30 min of the 3 hours (Harriet 10 min on pie alone).
2. **Establishing Question is singular** ("You met one of the Krause children") but there are three players. I asked each player; every Maven got her own child (Dot/David, Irene/Sara, Connie/Emily). Rules text ("whichever Krause child a Maven said") is fine for one Maven; for three it needs a line.
3. **No mechanical text for Crowns of the Void, what Conditions do, or what the Void thread's "offer a Crown" means.** I invented: Crown 1/7 lets Irene ask the water one question per session and get one true image. Also invented "Unsettled" as a Condition; the seed gives example Conditions only.
4. **No guidance for when a Maven reaches in the water: a roll? automatic?** I called a Composure roll when something brushed the boat (miss, Keeper move) and then let the reach go through anyway. Void Thread text says "when a Maven reaches for what it offers, offer a Crown" so no roll was required for the Crown itself.
5. **No in-world calendar.** Hook says "several nights ago", backstory says "three nights ago", happenings say Saturday/Thursday/Friday and "this week". I ruled: death = Sunday night; hook = Wednesday late afternoon; Sheriff deadline = noon Friday; Hollis vote = Thursday evening. Needed to invent this at the table.

## What worked
- Town Life First: starting each scene with the NPC's "life right now" produced the best role-play (Bev + wedding cake; Hollis + speech; Pelletier + Nadine; Gil + pies). No-one asked for a clue directly in the first three scenes.
- Free recap in S8 and the "one-line recap at each cut" kept the shared list honest.
- Players splitting themselves; no turn order was ever needed. Bea's boredom was the only friction and was solved by giving her a rival (Bunny) and a threatening NPC (Sorensen) on a miss.
- Misses as Keeper moves: Void sign in a cozy moment (v1), the rival (Bunny), the oar and the voice. All three were more memorable than the successes.
- The Void thread is tight: bearing, logbook, dusk, boat, voice, Crown. Leon wanted it badly and it paid.

## What did not
- **Not enough time for clues.** 3 hours with a 30 min session zero, 12 scenes and about 12 min per scene gave 8 roll-driven clues + Duchess, no Theorize. The seed's "10-12 clues by Theorize" plus complexity 8 plus slice-of-life suggestions don't fit in 3 hours.
- **Bea won't leave the rug alone** (c1) and neither Etienne's cold store nor the safe was reached, so the main physical clue chains (c1, c23, c15, c21, c17, c18) never appeared. If a theory had been rolled, they would have counted 5-6 clues against -8.
- Table knowledge leaks: Leon announced Irene knows about spirals before she'd heard it; I asked him to play it ignorant, and he did. The rule "players only know what's said aloud" makes everything shared and requires offscreen conversation fixes (Connie told Irene the numbers).
- r-split ("cut between groups every exchange or two") conflicts with r-townlife ("don't cut away the moment a personal beat starts"). I let scenes run 9-14 minutes and cut once each; Bea wanted shorter, Harriet longer.
