## 0:00 — Session zero, pitch, character creation  (~30 min)

**KEEPER:** Before we start, let me tell you how tonight works, in my own words. Brindlewood Bay is a slice-of-life game that happens to have a murder in it. Your Mavens are retired, nosy, underestimated and *busy*: they've got errands, feuds, hobbies, grandkids and love lives that don't stop because a millionaire went into the bay. So take the initiative. There are no rounds, no turn order. If something grabs you, say so, interrupt each other, jump in. When you push on something somebody wants hidden, you roll 2d6 plus one of your five abilities and a success earns a clue. Clues aren't hidden in fixed spots and nothing is decided yet; at the end you'll put together a theory and if the dice back it, your theory *is* what happened. Some clues will be strange, not just suspicious. Those are Void clues, part of something bigger under this town. Split up whenever you like, I'll cut between you. Lines and veils? X-card on the table, anybody can step out any time.

**OOC (Harriet):** Lines — nothing happening to kids or animals on screen. Veil the dog stuff, please. Is it a sad murder?
**KEEPER:** Duchess the dog is *missing*, and I promise you nothing happens to her on screen. Noted: dog is a line.
**OOC (Leon):** Mine's fine. Bring the creepy.
**OOC (Bea):** Can we just start? How long is character creation?
**KEEPER (gm aside):** *(Seed has no character-creation rules; see gm-notes. Improvising a quick array.)* Okay: each of you is a Maven. Name, age, what you did, your cozy activity, and one personal stake in town: a rival, an old flame, a grudge, a grandchild. Then assign these to your five abilities: +2, +1, 0, 0, -1 (Vitality, Composure, Reason, Presence, Sensitivity).

*(Table chatter, ~25 minutes: Harriet takes ten minutes over the pie she's going to bake and a name; Leon asks if there's a character-sheet with Void stats; Bea has her whole thing done in four minutes and starts tapping.)*

### The Mavens

Abilities: Vitality 0, Composure +1, Reason 0, **Presence +2**, Sensitivity -1.
- **Irene Voss** (Leon) — 69, retired science teacher, widow of Walter, who was Gil Wexford's assistant keeper at Brindle Point lighthouse for fifteen years. *Cozy activity: tide-pooling and birding.* Stake: Walter's old logbooks are in her cellar, and she has never read the last one. Abilities: Vitality -1, Composure 0, Reason +1, Presence 0, **Sensitivity +2**.
- **Constance "Connie" Atwood** (Bea) — 66, retired court stenographer, fastest typist in three counties. *Cozy activity: the Times crossword, in pen.* Stake: her bridge rival **Bunny Hallworth** has joined the yacht crowd and will "just happen" to turn up. Abilities: Vitality -1, **Composure +1**, **Reason +2**, Presence 0, Sensitivity 0 (Bea: "I'm not taking Presence, people should just listen.").

## 1:00 — Scene 3: The Gull & Anchor, Dot  (~14 min)
