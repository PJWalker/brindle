## Prep gaps
1. **No character creation.** No attribute spread, no cozy-activity rules, no Maven playbooks, no starting Conditions, no XP/advance list. I improvised.
2. **No pacing/calendar for the in-game days.** Which day is the loft meeting? When can a Maven see Finch (9am)? What happens Friday? I set Wed 3pm → Thu dusk. The Festival is "Saturday" in some places and the Ball "next week" in others.
3. **Bev, the Krauses, Tiago, Prentiss and Hale have no "life right now"** while r-townlife says "Every NPC has 'Their life right now'". I had to invent Bev's from the happenings. Bev is the very example the rule text uses ("Bev is shouting at the chowder supplier") but nothing in the seed says that.
4. **c16 gates the Void thread.** The lighthouse bearing needs c16 (a slip of numbers), which sits in Emily's things, the fish market or Hollis's log margin — places my players hadn't reached. So Gil's logbook was a dead end. I improvised a vague bearing.
5. **No guidance when a clue is half-earned** (e.g., c23's "fresh smear of white paint": a sighting vs. a physical look). I gave the sighting and the skiff in view.
6. **Mechanical effect of Conditions** is "check your rulebook". I gave Rattled and didn't use it.
7. **T2 "tick at scene changes"** — every scene change would end it in three scenes. I ticked it once, on a miss at the fish market, and noted that I hadn't followed the rule as written.

## Instruction check
- *Slice of life / initiative:* worked best with the personal hooks (Lou, Vivian, Finch's dahlias). Maureen needed one hook; once given she asked for scenes.
- *No rounds / scenes:* worked as scene framing, but with three players each driving their own Maven, the spotlight moved in order whether or not I wanted it to. Interrupts were rare; the other two sat as audience and cheered. I'd note that "split up" and "interrupt" fight each other.
- *Splitting:* nearly every scene was a single Maven. The only time they were together was the loft at the start and end.
- *Non-mystery challenges:* Lou/Nadine, Vivian, Ida's tea, the Harbour vote, the tea cosy and the dog. About 40% of the scenes. They kept the table laughing and every one of them delivered a clue or a threat sideways, so the "distract on purpose" scenes weren't free of the case; I'd call that the seed's best design.
- *Void:* one cozy moment (v3) and one at dusk (v4). Never offered a Crown because the thread needs c16.
