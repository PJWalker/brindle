## 0:00 — Arrival, snacks, pitch (0:00–0:12)

Chairs scraped, tea poured, someone brought lemon bars. Dev is already doing a voice. Maureen asks quietly if she needs to "know the rules first." Keeper says no.

**K (pitch, own words):** "Okay, here's the deal. You're three elderly ladies in a very pretty fishing town, and you have a book club that is secretly a murder-solving club. Think of it as a slice of life. Your Mavens have lives — errands, feuds, hobbies, grandchildren, a cat that needs the vet — and the murder happens *in* those lives, not instead of them. Nobody takes turns. There's no going around the table. If something grabs you, just say 'I go over there' and do it. Interrupt each other, interrupt me. I'll tell you when something needs dice. When you want to push on someone who'd rather not tell you something, you roll two dice plus one of your five abilities, and a good roll earns you a clue. Clues aren't hidden in secret spots; nothing's decided yet. When you've got enough, you put a theory together, and if the dice agree, your theory is what happened. Some clues will just be *weird* — those are Void clues, part of something under the town, and you can weave them in if you like. And you can split up. I'll cut between you."

**Dev (OOC):** "So I can just wander off? Like, no mystery reason? I just want to see what's in the diner?"
**K:** "Absolutely. The diner is exactly where I'd wander."
**Ruth (OOC):** "Do I get to keep notes of the clues?"
**K:** "Yes. I'll say 'that's a clue' out loud when you earn one, and you write it on a card."
**Maureen (OOC, small):** "What if I don't know what my character would do?"
**K:** "Then she can do what *you* would do on a Tuesday. That's usually right."

**Safety (0:10):** K asks for lines and veils. Maureen: no harm to animals on-screen. Dev: none. Ruth: no graphic detail on children being hurt. Veils: body stuff stays offscreen/fade-to-black. X-card explained; the table nods.
