# Player p3 interview: Margaret (Murder, She Wrote superfan)

Interviewed separately with say.sh after Stars & Wishes, out of character. Answers verbatim (lightly trimmed).

## Main questions

Out of character now, and thank you for a fun evening.

**1. Best moment:** The library. "Just one more thing" to David, and then Raj's Harriet reading the tape back flat as court. It felt like the real thing. A close second was inventing the Hendersons' car from the greenhouse stool. I'd put the stool there for flavor, and then it became a Clue.

**2. Confusion:** Quite a bit, honestly. Advantage was fine. Crowns I understood only when I used one, and I didn't know the cost until after I said yes. Conditions were vague: when does "Guilty" actually hurt me? A Cozy Little Place and the Cozy Move I understood by the end. Theorize I followed mostly because you walked us through it step by step.

**3. What the game wanted:** For us to *make things up together*, and I realized around the Emily scene, when you asked what Winnie remembered. It's not a puzzle with a hidden answer. It's more like Murder, She Wrote written by committee, and I liked that better than I expected.

**4. Theorize:** It felt earned socially, because we argued and compromised. But the maths felt too generous. With fourteen Clues, snake eyes still hit. Also, the rule that we could *add details* to make Clues fit felt like cheating at first. It's the opposite of how Jessica plays, but I came around.

**5. The Void:** I enjoyed them, and the shadows around the yacht were properly creepy. Walter's card confused me, though. I couldn't tell whether it was meant to be understood yet. The kneelers fit the cozy-with-teeth tone, and I'm more curious than frightened.

**6. Pacing:** I got plenty of spotlight, maybe slightly more than the others during the Theorize. The cozy and danger balance worked. The Sheriff scene and the market were my favorites.

**7. Leads I wanted:** The unlit skiff at 12:15, Etienne's debt to Albert, Emily's journalist, and Gil the lighthouse keeper. I'd also have liked to see the "H" drawer.

**8. One change:** A real red herring. Nobody on the suspect list turned out innocent, and I missed that.

**9. Again?** Yes, happily, and I'd bring my own pie.

## Follow-up

**Q:** You said Walter's card confused you. What would have helped, or would you rather it stayed mysterious?

I'd rather it stayed mysterious. A card in Walter's handwriting saying "Don't write it down, Dot. It reads back" is exactly the sort of thing I want to keep chewing on all week. If you'd explained it, I'd have been disappointed.

What confused me wasn't the *content*, it was the *handling*. You told us the Void Clues wouldn't solve the murder, which helped. But I didn't know what to do with it as a player. Should Winnie react? Should I ask questions? Winnie is a rationalist who doesn't believe in any of it, so I had nowhere to put it except "it was the fog." I think what would have helped is a small hook to pull on afterward. One concrete thing, like the K drawer, and then we leave the rest in the dark.

I'd also say that Walter's card was *Dot's* scene, so I was watching from the side. If Imogen had said "Winnie, come look at this," I'd have understood how to be part of it. The scary part worked better when it was shared at the table than when it was Dot alone, where I mostly felt like the audience.

So: stay mysterious, give us one thing to hold, and let the other Mavens get a look.
