# Player interview: Benny (p3), played Dolly Pennywhistle

g5, Dad Overboard, convention one-shot. Interviewed one-to-one after the session, out of character. Answers verbatim.

## Questions

1. What was your best moment?
2. Was there anything you didn't understand: the moves, the dice, advantage, Crowns, Conditions, the Cozy Little Place, Theorize?
3. What did you think the game wanted from you, and when did you realise it? Did my opening "how to play" pitch (go anywhere; Meddling finds Clues; Day/Night; Theorize at half the Complexity, and a hit makes it true; Void Clues; Crowns) help, and was anything in it wrong or missing?
4. Theorize: did it feel fair? Earned? Did you understand what counted as a Clue being "explained"?
5. The Void Clues (the moving tattoo, the chanting on the voicemail, the kneelers, the purple light): what did you make of them?
6. Pacing and spotlight: did you get enough? Did anyone dominate? How was the balance between cozy and danger? And the ending was cut short: how did that feel?
7. Were there leads you wanted to follow and couldn't?
8. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
9. Would you play again?

## Answers

1. **Best moment:** Dolly's Clue 12+ on the forecastle, "you've scrubbed the wrong patch of deck", with Allison just sitting down on the teak. But honestly the best moment was before that: the kitchen scene, where Dolly said out loud that Walter never told her about 1998, and Dot gave us the Harold line. I didn't expect a murder-mystery game to get that quiet.

2. **Confusing bits:** Mostly fine. Things that took me a few tries: (a) which ability to use for Meddling. I only got that it depends on how you do it once you said it twice. (b) Crowns: I understood the bump, but not what the Queen boxes really cost until you explained the scene owed. (c) Advantage versus disadvantage stacking, the Biscuit and Caught Snooping thing. I guessed it cancelled and got lucky. (d) The Cozy Move: I never fully got why Dot and Marjorie got Clues too until you said "both doing their own hobby."

3. **What the game wanted:** I think it wanted us to build the story together and treat Clues as raw material, not answers. I realised when you said "nothing is decided, not even by me." Your opening pitch helped a lot, especially "a hit makes it true." What was missing: Theorize can't be boosted by Gold Crown or advantage (Wendy had to be told), and "explained away" only came up at the end. I'd put those up front.

4. **Theorize:** Fair and earned. 12 Clues felt like a lot, but I understood "explained" once we talked through every Clue. I liked that the table had to agree. The only worry: I pitched a lot, and I wonder if Chris and Wendy felt steamrolled into my version. I tried to compromise.

5. **Void Clues:** Loved them, especially the tattoo moving and the kneelers. I assumed they were for next time, and the Midwives scene confirmed it. I'm curious about Walter's slip. Did you plan that, or was it improvised?

6. **Pacing and spotlight:** Pacing was good. Honest answer: I was the chattiest. I tried to hand off to Chris and Wendy, but I interrupted a few times. Cozy-versus-danger was a nice balance, and the ending being cut short was a bit abrupt, but the confrontation was still satisfying.

7. **Leads:** Walter's slip, the 1998 file, and the Prentiss voicemail chanting. Also Sorensen, who we never really confronted.

8. **Change one thing:** a one-page cheat sheet of Clues explained, so I wasn't tracking 12 in my head.

9. **Play again:** Absolutely. I want to know what Walter and Gerald knew.

## Follow-up

**Q:** Did Chris and Wendy feel steamrolled? When did you feel the spotlight, and did you ever hold back? Did your three early misses hurt your fun?

**A:**

**Spotlight:** Mostly during the theory talk and the opening. I'd pitch a whole plan, then ask Chris and Wendy what they thought, which really means I'd already filled the air. The "Dolly goes to County Records, Dot and Marjorie go to the yacht" split was me dividing the party. Nobody objected, but I didn't give them a real chance to. I also kept suggesting things for their Mavens, like "Dot, check on Emily" and "Marjorie, Columbo on the tea." It came from enthusiasm, but it's also being pushy.

**Held back:** Less than I should have, honestly. The one time I really held back was when Wendy was working through the odds, because I could see she liked that job. Otherwise, I didn't hold back much. I was the one who said "keep the miss, save the Crowns," which Wendy and Chris could've wanted to weigh in on.

**The misses:** They stung a bit at the moment. The Etienne one hurt most, because I'd just watched Dot and Marjorie roll 13 and 12. But they didn't hurt the fun. The miss gave us the orange man and the hurried hearse, and Caught Snooping gave me a reason to use Biscuit. Truthfully, I wasn't tempted to burn a Crown, partly because I liked the story it created.

I'd trust the dice more next time, and let others pitch first.
