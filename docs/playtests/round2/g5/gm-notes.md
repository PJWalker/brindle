# g5 Keeper notes: Dad Overboard, convention one-shot

Style under test: convention one-shot (strangers, hard 4-hour slot). Book's "Running Brindlewood Bay as a One-Shot": Session One procedure, then Theorize with ~30 min left (house target: exchange 40-45 of ~55) wherever things stand; on a miss, encourage every Maven to put on a Crown. Cut to Commercial once; explain Gold Crown Mysteries Move. Testing timing, how fast new players grasp moves/Theorize, and whether the app's "Say This at the Start" pitch (r-pitch) helps.

Players: p1 Chris (competitive), p2 Wendy (numbers/rules), p3 Benny (chatty).
Exchange counting: every Keeper message to the table = 1 exchange; a private say.sh aside or parallel set of asides = 1 exchange.

## Prep (before play)
- Read book sections listed in brief and seed.js (moves, rules, setting, mys-dad).
- Assumptions per brief: sheet = +1,+1,0,0,-1 then +1 to one (max +2); invented name/style/Cozy Activity; one classic Maven move each; EoS from app's `endOfSession` list (first + two others); Queen 5 boxes any order, Void 7 boxes in order (app crownsMax 7), improvised text; XP 5.
- Prep decisions (r-prep checklist used; it helped): taboo affair (c19) = Andrew & Allison (default); c16 numbers = study safe combination (default); c9 crossed-out name chosen when it lands; Void Clues planned: v3 tattoo (Finch / Tiago), v2 engine-room chanting, v4 prayer group at the Point. Cut to Commercial prompts ready: Possum-Be-Gone, DrinkPlus, VitaMagic. My Lines & Veils: Line sexual violence, Veil torture (book author's).
- Day/Night: daytime scenes at the market/docks = Day; yacht at night or the Point at dusk = Night.
