# g5 Keeper notes: Dad Overboard, convention one-shot

Style under test: convention one-shot (strangers, hard 4-hour slot). Book's "Running Brindlewood Bay as a One-Shot": Session One procedure, then Theorize with ~30 min left (house target: exchange 40-45 of ~55) wherever things stand; on a miss, encourage every Maven to put on a Crown. Cut to Commercial once; explain Gold Crown Mysteries Move. Testing timing, how fast new players grasp moves/Theorize, and whether the app's "Say This at the Start" pitch (r-pitch) helps.

Players: p1 Chris (competitive), p2 Wendy (numbers/rules), p3 Benny (chatty).
Exchange counting: every Keeper message to the table = 1 exchange; a private say.sh aside or parallel set of asides = 1 exchange.

## Final report

**One-line summary:** g5, convention one-shot (competitive Chris, numbers-minded Wendy, chatty Benny). The table named David (swung the heirloom vase) with Allison (held Albert's arm, ran the cover-up) and Andrew (cleaned), Sorensen squeezing them. 12 ordinary Clues (10 rolled or Columbo, 2 player-named via the Cozy Move) and 3 Void Clues. Theorize was +6 (12 Clues − Complexity 6) and rolled 9 (7–9). The confrontation used Cut to Commercial and the Gold Crown move. **Cut short at the organiser's request after 21 of a planned ~55 exchanges**, with Theorize and the confrontation already done.

**How far we got:** every Session One step ran: intros, CATS, safety, characters, Cozy Little Place, break with private Lines & Veils, EoS questions, vignettes, the mystery, investigation, Theorize at Ex18, confrontation, epilogue, Crown scenes (none outstanding), End of Session, Stars & Wishes, stinger, interviews. The cut cost only depth. The investigation was already done, and the confrontation, which I'd planned as two or three scenes, became one exchange.

### App guidance that helped
- **r-pitch ("Say This at the Start")**: net positive. All three players cite "a hit makes the theory true" as the line that told them what the game wanted, and they got the Theorize loop and the Crown rescue almost at once.
- **mv-theorize gm** (interrogate Clues, invite retcons, guide to consensus): this is what turned three competing theories into one at Ex16–17. **mv-meddle gm** ("every hit is a Clue; scan and pick") and **r-clues** (the corpse advice) made the fish-market 12+ easy to deliver.
- **mv-dice** ("advantage and disadvantage cancel") settled Biscuit vs. Caught Snooping in one line. **r-gmmoves** gave me the Cut to Commercial procedure and its prompts.
- NPC entries carried scenes on their own: Pelletier's quote, Etienne's apron tell, Sorensen's orange and "Death doesn't cancel a debt", Prentiss's "I couldn't possibly", Gil's dog and pies. The **Fixed Facts** (the 11:52 voicemail and the Sorensen launch times) let me answer timeline questions without improvising. **r-prep** was a good checklist.
- The note on c17 that it can surface other ways let me deliver the paternity Clue via Prentiss on the phone.

### Where the app contradicted the book, was wrong, or misled
- **r-pitch: "Put on a Crown … it costs you a scene."** Wendy took this as sitting out a scene, and Chris recalled the confusion in his interview. The cost is a box on the sheet, and for the Void it moves you toward retirement.
- **r-pitch omissions** the players had to learn mid-play, each costing an exchange: a Clue must be a thing in the world, not a deduction; "otherwise explained away" counts at Theorize; Theorize ignores advantage and other moves, so Gold Crown can't help it; anyone can roll, it needs consensus, and you can retry after a miss. All three players' "one change" was a one-page sheet covering exactly these.
- **mv-goldcrown** doesn't say it can't affect Theorize. Wendy planned around that, and the only warning is a line in mv-theorize.
- **Hook** says "several nights ago" and drops the book's "earlier today" and "soon, he'll be forced to let the family take Albert back." The urgency then has to come from t1/t2, which aren't presented to the players.
- **endOfSession + app.js "eos-award"** gives every Maven the same XP from one shared list. The book has each player answer her own three questions. Here it came out the same by luck.
- **mv-cozy gm** doesn't warn that one scene with several Mavens each doing her own Cozy Activity yields several free Clues. I gave two (and a third banked) in one scene at Ex10.
- **r-flow ONE-SHOT** ("Theorize with ~30 min left") assumes Clues are scarce. Here the trigger never came close: Theorize was viable at 95%+ by Ex14, about 2 hours into 4.
- No **Dark Conspiracy layer thresholds** anywhere, so the Layer One stinger was a judgement call.

### Gaps and errors in the Dad Overboard data
- **Captain Curly (Carl Willoughby)**, a published Suspect, is missing. Tiago is called "skipper" on loc-regal but "deckhand" on npc-tiago and in the Fixed Facts.
- **Duplicate event manager**: npc-lavoie (Prudence) and npc-hale (Philippa), same role. loc-inn and the c9 notes use Philippa.
- **npc-albert.hides "Gerald Pratt"** vs Gerald Prentiss everywhere else. **Andrew and Allison**: "twenty years" (npc-allison) vs 35 (npc-andrew, c19). **Sorensen**: "10pm–12:25" (npc-sorensen) vs 9:50 out and 12:20 radio (Fixed Facts). Wendy caught that timeline mismatch herself.
- **Content clashes with common Lines**: c15 (a rat stuffed in a cod), v1 and v5 (dying or dead fish), t4 step 1 (dead fish on the tide line), and c20/npc-duchess (blood in the dog's fur). A no-animal-harm Line removes three of six Void Clues and a threat step, and nothing in the data is tagged for content.
- **mys-dad.paint** lacks the book's back-of-the-fish-market question ("you have the feeling you're being watched"). I used it from the book and it set the tone well. The marina question has no location.
- **c16** is an ordinary Clue but reads as Void. New players classed it as Void and "explained it away" for +1. Its notes don't link it to the 1998 thread, which is where it played best (I made it Walter's handwriting).
- A 12+ Meddling can teach "something about the dark conspiracy", but the mystery has no Layer 1 material for that. I improvised the 1998 clipping from npc-nadine.hides and loc-gazette.

### Rules friction for new players
- They learned Meddling very fast. Choosing the ability took two or three rulings each ("which ability, and the odds?").
- Crowns: understood after a full explanation. Two of three refused small Crowns to save them for the Theorize rescue; the one-shot rescue rule shaped how they spent Crowns from the start.
- Cozy Little Place: never used for advantage by anyone. Chris says he'd only notice it written in front of him. Conditions: only three handed out, one erased by a Crown. Book target is 1–2 per Maven.
- Clue vs. deduction, and "explained away", both needed rulings. With table.sh replying in parallel, "go in turn" doesn't work, so collisions happen (two Dots, two crossword fans, two Cozy Moves aimed at a Condition that had just been erased).

### Verdict on the convention one-shot style
The structure works and fits comfortably in four hours: setup took about 6 exchanges, and the whole arc closed at Ex20 even after the cut. The **30-minute Theorize trigger was never needed**. At Complexity 6 with book-generous Meddling, two 12+ results and a three-Mavens Cozy Move, the table had 8 Clues by Ex12 and 11 by Ex14, and asked to Theorize at about the halfway point. The "encourage every Maven to Crown on a miss" rule mattered anyway: knowing the net was there made players save Crowns all game, and on the 7–9 they chose the hard confrontation over a unanimous Crown. Cut to Commercial once (on a perilous night miss) and the Gold Crown move (used to settle the paternity question) were both highlights and easy to teach in two lines. For cons I'd raise the Complexity or cap Clues: the decision to Theorize was the most tense moment, and Wendy and Chris both said they wanted that choice to carry real risk.

### Top 5 recommended changes
1. **Rewrite r-pitch.** Replace "it costs you a scene" with "mark a box: a Queen box owes a personal scene; a Void box moves you toward retirement." Add: "A Clue is a thing in the world, not a deduction. Theorize needs everyone to agree, anyone rolls, Clues you explain away also count, and nothing else modifies the roll. You can retry after a miss." Add one line each on the Cozy Move and on Cozy Little Place advantage.
2. **Add a one-shot pacing note to r-flow** for when Clues come too fast: "If Theorize is already 80%+ by mid-session, offer it, and spend the remaining time on a harder confrontation or the Void thread, or use the Clue-budget house rule." Also warn in mv-cozy that a multi-Maven Cozy scene can yield several Clues.
3. **Fix the data errors**: restore Captain Curly; merge npc-lavoie into npc-hale; change "Gerald Pratt" to Prentiss; reconcile Andrew's 20/35 years and Sorensen's times; make Tiago consistently deckhand.
4. **Tag content on clues and threat steps** (animal harm: c15, v1, v5, t4 step 1, the c20 notes) and add replacement Void Clues, so a Keeper can swap them after Lines & Veils without improvising.
5. **Make End of Session per Maven** (each Maven marks her own three questions, and eos-award applies them per Maven), and add a mv-goldcrown note: "can't affect Theorize; best used to declare a fact that fixes a hole in the theory."
